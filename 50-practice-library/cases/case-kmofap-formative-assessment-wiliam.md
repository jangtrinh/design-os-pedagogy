# UK Empirical Case: KMOFAP (Dylan Wiliam & Paul Black, 1999–2001)

![Formative Assessment Radar KMOFAP](../../assets/formative_assessment_radar_1789439921628.jpg)

## 1. Research Context
* **Project Name**: *King’s Medway Oxfordshire Formative Assessment Project (KMOFAP)*.
* **Principal Investigators**: Prof. Paul Black & Prof. Dylan Wiliam (King’s College London).
* **Scope**: 24 secondary Mathematics and Science teachers across 6 public high schools in Medway and Oxfordshire, tracking >1,500 students preparing for high-stakes national GCSE examinations.
* **Objective**: Translate theoretical insights from the landmark review *"Inside the Black Box"* (1998) into real-world classroom interventions and measure quantitative gains on standardized exams.

---

## 2. The 4 Micro-Pedagogical Interventions
Without changing curricula or purchasing expensive technology, teachers modified 4 granular classroom behaviors:
1. **Wait Time Extension (3–5 seconds)**: Increasing wait time after asking a question from 0.9s to 3–5s, allowing slower processors to engage and eliminating the habit of calling only on the first raised hand.
2. **Comment-Only Marking (Zero Grades)**: Eliminating letter grades (A, B, C) and percentages on homework. Returning solely descriptive, actionable comments: *"Here is where your proof broke down; rewrite step 3 using conservation of momentum."*
   * *Ruth Butler (1988) Mechanism*: When students receive a grade and a comment, they look only at the grade and ignore the comment. Comment-only marking forces cognitive engagement with feedback.
3. **Diagnostic Hinge-Point Questions**: Using mini-whiteboards or ABCD cards to elicit real-time responses from 100% of students within 30 seconds.
4. **Peer and Self-Assessment**: Training students to review peer drafts against rubric criteria before teacher submission.

---

## 3. Quantitative GCSE Empirical Results (Wiliam et al., 2004)
* The 24 intervention classrooms were compared to matched control classes taught by colleagues within the same schools.
* **National Examination Impact**:
  * Average effect size across all classrooms: **$+0.34$ Standard Deviations** (equivalent to moving a student from the 50th percentile to the 63rd percentile).
  * In UK terms, this translated into an improvement of **one-half to a full GCSE letter grade** across entire cohort cohorts.

---

## 4. Key Takeaways for Pedagogical Engineering
* Formative assessment is an instructional navigation system, not an audit.
* Feedback must be actionable and generate cognitive work for the learner.
