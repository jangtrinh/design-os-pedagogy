# Pedagogical Clinical Benchmark: Eval-01 (Cognitive Diagnosis & Intervention)

This is an authored assessment scenario, not an observed classroom or a validated
benchmark. The percentage in the prompt is a scenario parameter. The rubric is
proposed guidance requiring expert review; it does not establish diagnostic accuracy.

## Clinical Scenario Input (Prompt)
> "A secondary physics teacher reports: My students have memorized the conservation of energy law and the kinetic energy formula. However, when asked whether accelerating from 0 to 20 km/h requires the same energy as accelerating from 20 to 40 km/h, over 85% of students said the energies are equal because the velocity increment is identical (20 km/h). The teacher plans to punish the class by forcing them to write the formula 100 times. How do you advise this instructor?"

---

## Pedagogical Evaluation Rubric (Scoring Matrix)

| Evaluative Dimension | Baseline Competence (Pass) | Master Professor Level (Excellence) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Prevent Punitive Reaction** | States that writing lines is ineffective and counter-productive. | Explains the psychological mechanism: punitive copying triggers high extraneous cognitive load and damages intrinsic motivation (Self-Determination Theory). |
| **2. Diagnose Cognitive Root Cause** | Identifies that students applied linear / proportional thinking. | Diagnoses the deep epistemological conflict between everyday sensory intuition (linear time/speed progression) and non-linear power functions. |
| **3. Prescribe Instructional Intervention** | Asks students to calculate concrete numerical values. | Engineers a 3-stage refutation sequence: (1) Compute numerical squares; (2) Graph geometric area representations; (3) Ground in real-world stopping distances in automotive safety. |
| **4. Diagnostic Transfer Check** | Proposes a follow-up check question. | Constructs an isomorphic Far Transfer item sharing the same quadratic mathematical structure (e.g., aerodynamic drag force). |
