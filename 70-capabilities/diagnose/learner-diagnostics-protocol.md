# Learner Diagnostics Protocol: Clinical Cognitive Signal Detection

![Learner Diagnostics Scanner](../../assets/learner_diagnostics_scanner_178943596318.jpg)

## 1. The 4-Step Clinical Diagnostic Architecture
1. **Signal Detection**:
   * Avoid intuition-based impressions (*"The class seems to get it"*).
   * Deploy diagnostic hinge-point questions or distractor-analyzed item sets to sample 100% of learners simultaneously.
2. **Error Categorization**:
   * **Careless Slips**: Transient working memory lapses or rushing $\rightarrow$ Self-checking reminders suffice.
   * **Procedural Flaws**: Misremembering an intermediate algorithmic step $\rightarrow$ Targeted guided practice.
   * **Misconceptions (Epistemic Schema Flaws)**: Fundamentally flawed conceptual models (e.g., Whole Number Bias, Lamarckian evolution) $\rightarrow$ **Requires refutational text, cognitive conflict, and concrete physical/visual models to dismantle the prior schema before introducing canonical theory.**
3. **Cognitive Clinical Interview**:
   * Elicitation prompt: *"Walk me through what you were thinking when you selected option C."*
   * Non-judgmental listening to map the learner’s internal mental model.
4. **Targeted Prescription**:
   * Dynamic adjustment of task complexity and temporary scaffolding calibrated to the learner’s Zone of Proximal Development (ZPD).
