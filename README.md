## 🌌 Why This Repository Exists

Most educational resources merely **describe pedagogy**.  
`design-os-pedagogy` is engineered to **execute pedagogy as a verifiable operating system**.

Its foundational content unit is not a passive textbook chapter or a collection of definitions. In this system, any document containing only declarative facts is **incomplete by specification**. Every substantive module must operationalize the **Universal Pedagogical Authoring Specification**:

$$\text{Knowledge} + \text{Experience} + \text{Diagnosis} + \text{Practice} + \text{Feedback} + \text{Transfer} + \text{Protocol}$$

```text
Knowledge (Causal Mechanism)
  ↳ Experience (Intuitive Dilemma & Prediction Challenge)
    ↳ Diagnosis (Misconception Catalog & 4-Distractor Hinge Questions)
      ↳ Practice (Worked Examples & Cognitive Fading)
        ↳ Feedback (Informational & Formative Adaptation)
          ↳ Transfer (Near & Far Generalization Scenarios)
            ↳ Protocol (Turn-Key Dialogue Scripts & State Machines)
```

### The 2026 Epistemic Invariants

1. **The Invariant of Epistemic Authority**:
   $$\mathbf{AI\ may\ carry\ cognitive\ load,\ but\ it\ must\ not\ silently\ inherit\ epistemic\ authority.}$$
2. **The Independence of Competence and Assistance**:
   $$\mathbf{Assistance\ Level \ne Competence\ Level \quad\mid\quad Authorship \ne Mastery}$$
3. **The Empirical Grounding Standard**:
   Educational neuromyths (e.g., learning styles, left/right-brain dichotomies, the Mozart effect) are strictly rejected with refutational evidence gates. All pedagogical methods must trace directly to randomized controlled trials (RCTs) or systematic meta-analyses reporting sample sizes ($N$) and effect sizes (Cohen’s $d$ or Hedges’ $g$).

## ⚡ Turnkey Autonomous Research Pipeline CLI

The repository includes a self-contained, end-to-end command-line research and authoring pipeline (`./bin/pedagogy-pipeline` / [`tools/pedagogy_pipeline.py`](tools/pedagogy_pipeline.py)):

```bash
# 1. Run full end-to-end research, debate, synthesis, and MOC integration
./bin/pedagogy-pipeline "Cognitive Apprenticeship in Medical Clinical Education"

# 2. Run with high-reasoning model and auto-push to GitHub
./bin/pedagogy-pipeline "Historical Sourcing and Corroboration" -m high -p

# 3. Dry-run to preview topic classification, target directory, and prompt
./bin/pedagogy-pipeline --dry-run "Desirable Difficulties in Spaced Retrieval"

# 4. Audit all markdown links, images, and wikilinks across the repository
python3 tools/verify_links.py
```

### What the Pipeline Executes Automatically:
* **Topic Classification**: Deterministically routes concepts into `10-foundations/`, `20-stages/`, `30-pedagogy/`, `40-disciplines/`, `50-practice-library/`, or `70-capabilities/`.
* **Adversarial Codex Web Debate**: Formulates deep reasoning prompts enforcing empirical literature retrieval, effect sizes ($d, g$), and the Universal Authoring Specification.
* **Extraction & Clean Writing**: Strips runtime banners, validates the 11 core pedagogical components, and auto-embeds matching 3D isometric visuals from `assets/`.
* **Bidirectional Navigation Sync**: Automatically inserts `[[stem|Display Title]]` into the relevant Map of Content (`00-navigation/MOC-*.md`).
* **AST Link & Integrity Verification**: Executes [`tools/verify_links.py`](tools/verify_links.py) ensuring zero 404 image errors, zero broken relative links, zero unresolved wikilinks, and zero path leaks.

## 🔬 5 Landmark Empirical Experiments in Educational History

Every pedagogical intervention in this operating system is benchmarked against historic large-scale empirical experiments:

1. **Project Follow Through (USA, 1967–1977)**:
   * The largest educational RCT in history: \$1B, 79,000 disadvantaged students across 180 communities over 10 years. **Direct Instruction (DI)** outperformed all progressive, constructivist, and discovery models across basic skills ($+0.82$), cognitive conceptual skills ($+0.46$), and self-esteem ($+0.28$).
   * 📖 Case Study: [`50-practice-library/cases/case-project-follow-through-direct-instruction.md`](50-practice-library/cases/case-project-follow-through-direct-instruction.md)
2. **Harvard Conceptual Physics Crisis & Peer Instruction (1990–2001)**:
   * Prof. Eric Mazur discovered that traditional lectures create an illusion of competence. Replacing passive listening with 2-minute peer debates on ConcepTests doubled normalized learning gains ($g = 0.23 \rightarrow g = 0.74$, $N > 3,000$ Harvard undergraduates).
   * 📖 Case Study: [`50-practice-library/cases/case-eric-mazur-harvard-peer-instruction.md`](50-practice-library/cases/case-eric-mazur-harvard-peer-instruction.md)
3. **KMOFAP Formative Assessment Project (UK, 1999–2001)**:
   * Dylan Wiliam & Paul Black trained 24 secondary teachers to extend Wait Time to 3–5 seconds and replace grades with comment-only formative feedback, achieving an average **$+0.34\text{ SD}$ gain** on national GCSE examinations.
   * 📖 Case Study: [`50-practice-library/cases/case-kmofap-formative-assessment-wiliam.md`](50-practice-library/cases/case-kmofap-formative-assessment-wiliam.md)
4. **Perry Preschool 40-Year Longitudinal RCT (1962–2005)**:
   * 40-year tracking of 123 high-risk children: 2 years of active Plan-Do-Review preschool yielded a 65% vs 45% high school graduation rate, 19% reduction in arrests, and \$7.16–\$12.90 social return per \$1 invested (Nobel laureate James Heckman ROI analysis).
   * 📖 Case Study: [`50-practice-library/cases/case-perry-preschool-highscope-longitudinal.md`](50-practice-library/cases/case-perry-preschool-highscope-longitudinal.md)
5. **McMaster & Maastricht Medical Problem-Based Learning (1969–2006)**:
   * Howard Barrows replaced theoretical lecture halls with the 7-Jump clinical problem-solving routine, demonstrating superior 5-year diagnostic retention and clinical performance over didactic medical curricula.
   * 📖 Case Study: [`50-practice-library/cases/case-mcmaster-maastricht-pbl-medical.md`](50-practice-library/cases/case-mcmaster-maastricht-pbl-medical.md)
