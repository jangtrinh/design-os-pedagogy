# S2: Primary Pedagogy — Building Foundational Literacy and Numeracy (FLN)

## 1. Primary Imperative: Shifting from "Learning to Read" to "Reading to Learn"
Primary school learners must automate foundational mechanical skills (phonemic decoding, basic arithmetic facts) to liberate working memory bandwidth for higher-order reasoning.

## 2. Explicit Instruction Architecture (Archer & Hughes)
The systematic **I Do -> We Do -> You Do** scaffolding framework:
1. **I Do (Explicit Teacher Modeling)**:
   * The instructor performs the task while providing a transparent "Think-Aloud," articulating each procedural step and conceptual rationale.
2. **We Do (Guided Practice with Feedback)**:
   * The entire cohort solves parallel problems collectively. The teacher continuously checks for understanding, delivering immediate corrective feedback.
3. **You Do (Independent Practice Release)**:
   * Students transition to unassisted problem solving only after attaining a verified **80%+ success threshold** during guided practice.

## 3. Disciplinary Pillars of Primary Education

* **The Science of Reading**:
  * Systematic Synthetic Phonics combined with academic vocabulary building and fluency drills (Scarborough’s Reading Rope).
  * *Rejection of Three-Cueing*: Guessing words based on context pictures or first letters is strictly eliminated.

* **Concrete-Pictorial-Abstract (CPA) Mathematics**:
  * Physical manipulatives (C) $\rightarrow$ Visual Bar Model diagrams (P) $\rightarrow$ Abstract numerical formulas (A).
