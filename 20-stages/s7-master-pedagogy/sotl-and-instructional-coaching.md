# S7: Master Pedagogy & Professor Development — Teacher of Teachers

![Master Professor SoTL HUD Console](../../assets/master_professor_sotl_hud_1789441816312.jpg)

## 1. Scholarship of Teaching and Learning (SoTL - Ernest Boyer)
A distinguished professor does not rely on instructional intuition alone; they transform the lecture hall and seminar into an empirical laboratory:
* Formulating empirical questions regarding student learning and cognitive obstacles.
* Systematically collecting evidence (student work, audio-visual interaction logs, diagnostic hinge items).
* Analyzing outcomes against learning science literature.
* Publishing and peer-reviewing instructional findings across the academic community.

## 2. Clinical Instructional Micro-Coaching (Jim Knight)
Mentoring junior faculty through collaborative, non-judgmental inquiry:
1. **Phase 1: Video-Based Self-Reflection**: The instructor analyzes video of their own classroom. The coach asks: *"What did you notice about student engagement at minute 18 when you asked that question?"*
2. **Phase 2: Target Teaching Behavior Identification**: Selecting a high-leverage micro-skill (e.g., extending wait-time from 1s to 3–5s, or using non-verbal thumbs-up checking).
3. **Phase 3: Micro-Teaching Simulation & Feedback**: Iterative rehearsing in low-stakes peer simulations prior to live classroom execution.
