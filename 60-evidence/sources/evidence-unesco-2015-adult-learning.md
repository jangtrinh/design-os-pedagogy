# Adult learning has diverse purposes

The recommendation includes formal, nonformal and informal learning, encompassing basic skills, professional development and community participation. Its quality guidance includes contextualized provision, appropriate language, flexible pathways and recognition of prior learning.

**Design use:** Ask about the adult's goals, existing capabilities and access constraints. Professional experience in one subject does not establish competence in a new one.

**Scope read:** The official recommendation's named sections. This is a normative framework, not a trial proving that adults reject lectures, require workplace problems or share a single learning preference. Cultural definitions and educational roles matter; the document does not supply a universal course format.
