# Designing for learner variability

CAST organizes design options around engagement, representation, and action and expression, with learner agency as the goal. The current organizer uses Access, Support and Executive Function groupings.

**Design use:** Identify barriers to a specified learning goal and offer suitable ways to participate and demonstrate learning. Consider language, prior knowledge, accessible tools and graduated practice support.

**Scope read:** The official organizer, not every linked consideration's research bibliography. Stage tags describe possible design settings, not replicated effects in every age group. The framework does not certify material accessibility, replace individual accommodations, diagnose learners, or guarantee a uniform academic effect.
