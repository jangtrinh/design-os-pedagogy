# How much performance variance deliberate practice explains

The abstract reports a meta-analysis across the major domains in which deliberate practice had been studied. Deliberate practice explained 26% of the variance in performance for games, 21% for music, 18% for sports, 4% for education and less than 1% for professions. The authors conclude that deliberate practice is important, but not as important as had been argued.

**Design use:** Practice remains a central design lever. A promise that equal practice produces equal outcomes is not supported by this source.

**Scope read:** Official abstract only (Europe PMC mirror of the PubMed record), accessed 2026-10-29. Number of studies, practice measures, moderators and the identity of the unexplained variance were not read. The abstract does not attribute the remaining variance to talent; it shows only that measured deliberate practice leaves most of it unexplained. Later re-analyses and replies were not read.
