# Karpicke & Blunt (2011): retrieval practice versus concept mapping

## What the abstract states (read 2026-10-29)

- Practising retrieval produced greater gains in meaningful learning than
  elaborative studying with concept mapping.
- The advantage held with science-education texts, with comprehension and
  inference questions, and when the final test itself required building concept
  maps.
- The authors interpret this as support for retrieval-specific mechanisms.

## Use in this repository

Supports asking learners to reconstruct a definition or relation from memory
before checking a document, rather than only reorganizing open-book notes. A
published comment exists (PMID 22034416, Mintzes et al.); its argument was not
read in this run, so the finding is recorded without settling that debate.
