# Kornell, Hays & Bjork (2009): failed retrieval attempts followed by the answer

## What the abstract states (read 2026-10-29)

- Six experiments. Materials ensured retrieval attempts would fail: fictional
  general-knowledge questions (Experiments 1–2) and weak-associate guessing
  (Experiments 3–6); the rare correct guesses were excluded.
- In the test condition participants attempted an answer before seeing it; in
  the read-only condition question and answer appeared together.
- Unsuccessful retrieval attempts enhanced learning with both material types.

## Use in this repository

Supports opening a closure or revisit with a recall attempt even when the
learner may fail, provided the correct answer follows. The materials were
word-level and fictional facts; whether this extends to conceptual technical
definitions is an applicability question, not a finding of this source. No
Grade is assigned until appraisal.
