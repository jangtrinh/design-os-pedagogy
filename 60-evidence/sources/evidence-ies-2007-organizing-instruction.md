# Planning practice and study

The summary recommends spaced study, alternating worked examples with problem solving, connecting representations, selected uses of quizzing, and explanatory questions. Its evidence labels vary by recommendation; prequestions and several study-monitoring recommendations have weaker labels than retrieval through quizzes and explanatory questions.

**Design use:** Specify what learners retrieve or explain, when they revisit it, and what feedback follows. A study schedule is a design hypothesis to evaluate.

**Scope read:** Official HTML recommendations and details only. The downloadable guide and underlying studies were not read or reappraised in this run. Published WWC labels are not converted into the repository's appraisal grades. Stage tags follow the site's broad school/postsecondary indexing, not proof of every method in every grade. No optimal universal spacing interval or effect size is asserted.
