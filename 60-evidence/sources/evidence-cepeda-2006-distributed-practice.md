# Cepeda et al. (2006): spacing, lag and retention interval

## What the abstract states (read 2026-10-29)

- A meta-analysis of 839 assessments of distributed practice, in 317
  experiments from 184 articles, on verbal recall tasks.
- It examined spacing (massed versus spaced episodes), lag (less versus more
  spaced) and expanding inter-study intervals.
- The inter-study interval and the retention interval act jointly: the interval
  giving the best retention grew as the retention interval grew.

## Use in this repository

Supports scheduling revisits rather than massed rereading, and choosing the gap
from how long the learner must retain the material. It does not supply a
universal schedule; the materials were verbal recall tasks, and transfer to
technical-skill learning is an applicability question. No Grade is assigned
until appraisal.
