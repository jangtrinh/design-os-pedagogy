# A critique of mirror-neuron accounts of action understanding

The abstract presents a critical review of the theory that mirror neurons provide the basis of action understanding. It argues that no monkey data directly test the theory and that evidence from humans makes a strong case against it.

**Design use:** Justify modelling and demonstration by what learners can do afterwards, not by a mirror-neuron mechanism.

**Scope read:** Official abstract only, accessed 2026-10-29. The eight arguments and responses from other researchers were not read. The review addresses action understanding; it does not test or deny that people learn by observing others. Hickok's later book was not read in this run.
