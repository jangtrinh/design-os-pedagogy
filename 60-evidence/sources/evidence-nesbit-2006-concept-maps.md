# Concept and knowledge maps: meta-analytic abstract

**Extracted from the abstract:** the review covers studies in which students learned
by constructing, modifying or viewing node-link diagrams. Learners ranged from
Grade 4 to postsecondary, in domains such as science, psychology, statistics and
nursing; posttests measured recall and transfer. Concept-map use was associated
with increased knowledge retention across several conditions and settings. Mean
effects ranged from small to large depending on how maps were used and on the
comparison treatment, with significant heterogeneity in most subsets.

**Scope read:** abstract only. Whether learner-constructed maps outperform studying
an expert map is a subgroup question this record does not answer; consult the
full-text tables before making that comparison.

**Applicability limit:** node-link maps are one kind of organizer. Tables, lists or
summaries written by the learner are related but different formats; treat any
extension to them as an authored design inference.
