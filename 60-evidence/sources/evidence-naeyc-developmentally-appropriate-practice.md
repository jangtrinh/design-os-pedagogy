# Developmentally appropriate practice

NAEYC describes development as variable across children, contexts and domains. Its guidance includes responsive interaction, play and intentional teaching. Neither exclusively self-directed play nor predominantly direct instruction constitutes a complete program.

**Design use:** Observe the individual child and consult caregivers before choosing supports. Preserve play and meaningful content learning together; adjust from observed responses.

**Scope read:** The named official HTML sections. The birth-through-eight scope overlaps only part of S2. The cited primary studies were not individually appraised. This statement does not establish universal attention durations, a fixed working-memory capacity, a diagnosis from behavior, or the causal effect of one preschool routine on adult economic outcomes.
