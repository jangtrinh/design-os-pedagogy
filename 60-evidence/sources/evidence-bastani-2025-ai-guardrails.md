# AI guardrails and subsequent independent performance

The abstract reports that the GPT Base group had lower unassisted exam grades
than the no-AI group after access was removed. The relative reduction was 17%;
safeguards in GPT Tutor largely mitigated this negative effect.

This is a task-performance finding, not neural atrophy, a Cohen's d value or a
guarantee for another tutor. Source extraction is checked; methodological
appraisal and generalization remain unreviewed.
