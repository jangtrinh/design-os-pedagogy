# Course alignment

The Eberly Center connects the intended performance, the assessment that elicits it, and the practice that prepares learners for it. Its examples distinguish recalling information from analyzing, evaluating and creating.

**Design use:** For each outcome, identify a matching assessment and preparation task. A polished artifact does not establish that the intended skill was assessed.

**Scope read:** The official HTML page, including its examples. This is institutional design guidance, not a controlled intervention or an effect-size estimate. Applying it outside higher education is an authored adaptation requiring context review. No publication date was inferred from the website copyright.
