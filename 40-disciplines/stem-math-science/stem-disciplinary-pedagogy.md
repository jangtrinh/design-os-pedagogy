# STEM Disciplinary Pedagogy & Empirical Science Instruction

![STEM NGSS 3D Science Learning & CER Modeling](../../assets/stem_ngss_cer_modeling_1789442801886.jpg)

## 1. Next Generation Science Standards: 3D Science Learning (NGSS)
1. **Science and Engineering Practices (SEPs)**:
   * Science is not acquired as dead definitions from a textbook.
   * Learners actively engage in disciplinary practices: Asking testable questions, developing & using models, planning investigations, analyzing data, and engaging in argument from evidence (CER Framework).
2. **Crosscutting Concepts (CCCs)**:
   * Cause and effect: Mechanism and explanation.
   * Structure and function.
   * Systems and system models.
   * Energy and matter: Flows, cycles, and conservation.
3. **Disciplinary Core Ideas (DCIs)**:
   * Deep organizing principles across Physical, Life, Earth, and Space Sciences.

## 2. Scientific Argumentation: The CER Framework (Claim - Evidence - Reasoning)
Every scientific explanation produced by a student must adhere to three rigorous components:
* **Claim**: A direct, testable proposition addressing the research question.
* **Evidence**: Empirical, measurable data, quantitative observations, or experimental metrics.
* **Reasoning**: The underlying scientific law or causal mechanism explaining *why* the empirical data logically necessitates the claim.

## 3. Singapore Math Bar Modeling
* **Visual Representation**: Utilizing proportional rectangular bars to represent known and unknown quantities.
* Bridges concrete physical counters to abstract symbolic algebra, allowing learners to decompose complex multi-step word problems into transparent geometric relationships.
