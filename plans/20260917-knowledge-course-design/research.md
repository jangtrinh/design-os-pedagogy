# Research record: course-design foundations

Research and source-check date: 17 September 2026.

## Question and selection

Which reusable primary guidance can correct the current course-design entry points and support learner-sensitive course authoring? The audit identified gaps in alignment, age/proficiency distinctions, access, adult learning and evidence-informed practice planning.

Discovery themes were course alignment, developmentally appropriate practice, UDL 3.0, adult learning and organizing study. Verification used the official URLs below. This is a targeted foundation review, not a systematic literature review or an appraisal of every cited study.

| Source | Discovery/verification URL | Reading scope and decision |

| CMU Eberly Center | https://www.cmu.edu/teaching/assessment/basics/alignment.html | Official HTML alignment questions, misalignment examples and assessment table; accepted as institutional design guidance; publication date not inferred |

Five source records were added because the two NAEYC pages belong to one framework record. Source dates and access dates are separate. Source checking identifies the AI extraction and does not imply an independent human review.

## Limits and material not promoted

The IES downloadable guide and underlying studies were not read or appraised in this run. The CAST consideration bibliographies and NAEYC's cited studies were not individually appraised. No effect estimate was extracted from these framework pages. The previously listed Wiggins/McTighe, Biggs, Knowles, Perry and other historical references were not newly verified merely because their names appeared in a local guide.

Source recommendations were separated from authored examples and implementation choices. Adult professional experience was not treated as universal subject expertise. Age tags were not treated as developmental diagnoses. The preschool examples do not inherit outcomes or economic returns from whole-program evaluations. The IES evidence labels were not converted automatically into the repository's appraisal grades.

## Changes resulting from the research

The CAP-02 protocol now includes an explicit brief, subject-source map, prerequisite sequence, alignment, practice, access and review contract. The early-childhood, adult-learning and UDL guides now use authored scenarios and follow-up observations instead of universal learner profiles. Backward-design and navigation claims were narrowed to what the checked guidance supports; broader subject/theoretical material remains unreviewed.

New operational artifacts include the blueprint template, course-design assistant, research-cycle protocol, acceptance scenarios and coverage reporting. These are authored tools for later review, not empirical findings.

## Unresolved questions

The remaining legacy evidence needs source-by-source appraisal. Topic-specific curricula, quotations, worked examples and assessment validity require subject review. The new prompt has not been run as a cross-model benchmark, and no learner pilot or learning-effectiveness evaluation was conducted. These limits remain visible in the source cards, authored records and roadmap.
