# Diagnostic Hinge-Point Questions (Dylan Wiliam)

![KMOFAP Formative Wait Time Chronometer](../../assets/kmofap_wait_time_chronometer_1789443291874.jpg)

## 1. Definition & Core Architecture
* A diagnostic check administered at the central pivot point of a lesson where the instructor must decide: Has the cohort understood sufficiently to advance, or must we pause and reteach?
* **Mandatory Design Criteria**:
  1. Students require $\le 2$ minutes to reflect and commit to an answer.
  2. The instructor collects and diagnoses responses from **100% of students within 30 seconds** (via mini-whiteboards, digital polling, or finger-voting).
  3. Every incorrect distractor isolates a specific **preconceived misconception**; no distractor is a generic or absurd filler.

## 2. Authentic Disciplinary Hinge Question Example
**Question**: *"Which scenario represents the most accurate example of Natural Selection?"*
* A. Giraffes stretched their necks to reach high tree foliage, subsequently birthing offspring with longer necks. *(Lamarckian acquired-traits misconception)*
* B. Individuals possessing random phenotypic variations that confer higher survival leave more offspring in the population. *(Correct Newtonian/Darwinian answer)*
* C. The environment compelled organisms to mutate intentionally to avoid extinction. *(Teleological purposeful-evolution misconception)*
* D. Animals actively decided to rewrite their DNA code to survive the sudden onset of an ice age. *(Intentional genetic modification misconception)*

## 3. Decision-Action Protocol Based on Poll Data
* **> 80% Correct**: Advance to independent application.
* **30% – 70% Correct**: Trigger a 2-minute **Peer Debate** (find a peer with a contrary choice and justify your schema) $\rightarrow$ Revote.
* **< 30% Correct**: Full instructional halt! Pivot to an alternative representation, provide a fresh concrete worked example, or draw a multi-modal diagram.
