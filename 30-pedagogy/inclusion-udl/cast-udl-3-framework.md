# Universal Design for Learning (CAST UDL 3.0)

![CAST UDL 3.0 Tri-Network Accessibility Nexus](../../assets/cast_udl_3_tri_network_1789442097694.jpg)

## 1. Foundational Axiom: Disability is Located in the Instructional Design, Not in the Learner
* Neurodiversity (including ADHD, Autism, Dyslexia, and varied sensory profiles) is a normal biological variation of the human connectome.
* Supreme Goal of UDL: Developing **Expert Learners** who are purposeful & motivated, resourceful & knowledgeable, strategic & goal-directed.

## 2. The 3 Brain Networks & The 3 UDL Pillars

### Pillar 1: Multiple Means of Representation
* *The "What" of Learning (Recognition Networks - Occipital and Temporal lobes)*:
  * Offer customizable display of information (adjustable font size, high-contrast themes).
  * Dual coding: synchronize auditory explanations with visual diagrams.
  * Explicitly pre-teach specialized domain vocabulary before encountering text passages.

### Pillar 2: Multiple Means of Action & Expression
* *The "How" of Learning (Strategic Networks - Prefrontal cortex)*:
  * Provide multi-modal output media: written essays, oral presentations, code repos, physical prototypes.
  * Scaffold executive functions with planning templates, milestone checklists, and self-monitoring timers.

### Pillar 3: Multiple Means of Engagement
* *The "Why" of Learning (Affective Networks - Limbic system)*:
  * Empower authentic student choice regarding task context and workspace setup.
  * Establish psychological safety where errors are analyzed constructively as empirical data.
