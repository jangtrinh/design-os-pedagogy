# MOC: Learning Sciences & Cognitive Neuroscience

![Paivio Dual Coding Convergent Optical Prism](../assets/dual_coding_prism_1789440523520.jpg)

This map synthesizes the biological, cognitive, and neurological principles governing how the human brain encodes, consolidates, and retrieves information.

---

## 1. Core Cognitive Foundations & Neurobiology
1. [[neurobiology-of-learning|Neurobiology of Learning]]: Long-Term Potentiation (LTP), NMDA receptors, CaMKII/CREB signaling, and sleep-dependent synaptic consolidation.
2. [[neuromyths-debunked|Neuromyths Debunked]]: Empirical refutation of VAK learning styles, the left-brain/right-brain dichotomy, and the 10% brain myth.
3. [[cognitive-load-theory|Cognitive Load Theory]]: Working memory capacity constraints (4±1 chunks), intrinsic/extraneous/germane loads, and long-term memory schema construction.
4. [[executive-functions|Executive Functions]]: The three core pillars (Inhibitory Control, Working Memory, Cognitive Flexibility).
5. [[spaced-practice-retrieval|Spaced Practice & Retrieval Practice]]: The testing effect ($g = 0.74$), Bjork's storage vs retrieval strength, and optimal inter-session lag schedules.
6. [[interleaving-practice|Interleaving Practice]]: Discriminative contrast ($d = 0.76$), category boundary induction, and eliminating blocked practice illusions.
7. [[achievement-motivation-and-agency|Achievement Motivation & Agency]]: Weiner's attribution theory, Bandura's self-efficacy, Eccles' expectancy-value, and reframing Dweck's growth mindset.
8. [[epistemic-ethics-and-critical-pedagogy|Epistemic Ethics & Critical Pedagogy]]: Gert Biesta's threefold teleology (qualification, socialization, subjectification), Freire's banking concept, and Miranda Fricker's epistemic injustice.
9. [[prenatal-neurodevelopment|Prenatal Neurodevelopment]]: Intrauterine neurodevelopmental optimization and maternal-fetal HPA stress axis modulation.
10. [[self-determination-theory|Self-Determination Theory]]: The triad of intrinsic motivation (Autonomy, Competence, Relatedness).
11. [[cognitive-offloading-and-atrophy|Cognitive Offloading and Atrophy]]: Generative AI as cognitive scaffold vs generator of epistemic debt and synaptic disuse (Bastani et al. 2024 PNAS, $d = -0.32$).
12. [[dual-coding-theory-multimedia-learning|Dual Coding Theory & Multimedia Learning]]: Paivio's dual coding channels and Mayer's 12 multimedia design principles.

## 2. Linked Empirical Evidence & Syntheses
* [[meta-analytic-effect-size-synthesis|Meta-Analytic Effect Size Synthesis]]: Master ranking of 20 primary pedagogical interventions benchmarked against Hattie's $d = 0.40$ hinge point.
* [[pedagogical-evidence-map|Pedagogical Evidence Map]]: 2D Stage-by-Intervention matrix (S0–S7) with developmental effect sizes and boundary contraindications.
* [[evidence-barbieri-2023-worked-examples|Evidence — Barbieri et al. 2023 Worked Examples]]: Meta-analysis of 55 studies proving worked examples reduce cognitive load ($g = 0.48$).
* [[evidence-mawson-2025-spacing|Evidence — Mawson & Kang 2025 Spacing]]: Meta-analysis demonstrating that spaced practice enhances long-term retention ($d = 0.54$).
* [[evidence-agarwal-2021-retrieval|Evidence — Agarwal et al. 2021 Retrieval Practice]]: Systematic review of classroom retrieval practice across authentic school settings.
* [[cognitive-offloading-and-atrophy|Evidence — Bastani et al. 2024 Cognitive Atrophy]]: Experimental RCT proving immediate performance inflation with simultaneous 17% deficit on unassisted exams.
