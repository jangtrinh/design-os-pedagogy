# MOC: Learning Sciences & Cognitive Neuroscience

![Paivio Dual Coding Convergent Optical Prism](../assets/dual_coding_prism_1789440523520.jpg)

This map synthesizes the biological, cognitive, and neurological principles governing how the human brain encodes, consolidates, and retrieves information.

---

## 1. Core Cognitive Foundations
1. [[Cognitive Load Theory]]: Working memory capacity constraints (4±1 chunks) and long-term memory schema construction.
2. [[Executive Functions]]: The three core pillars (Inhibitory Control, Working Memory, Cognitive Flexibility).
3. [[Prenatal Neurodevelopment]]: Intrauterine neurodevelopmental optimization and maternal-fetal HPA stress axis modulation.
4. [[Self-Determination Theory]]: The triad of intrinsic motivation (Autonomy, Competence, Relatedness).

## 2. Linked Empirical Evidence
* [[Evidence — Barbieri et al. 2023 Worked Examples]]: Meta-analysis of 55 studies proving worked examples reduce cognitive load ($g = 0.48$).
* [[Evidence — Mawson & Kang 2025 Spacing]]: Meta-analysis demonstrating that spaced practice enhances long-term retention ($d = 0.54$).
* [[Evidence — Agarwal et al. 2021 Retrieval Practice]]: Systematic review of classroom retrieval practice across authentic school settings.

## 3. Actionable Pedagogical Translations
* From *Cognitive Load Theory* $\rightarrow$ Design [[Rosenshine 10 Principles]] and [[Worked Examples]].
* From *Executive Functions* $\rightarrow$ Design [[Play and Executive Function]] (Early Childhood) and [[CAST UDL 3.0 Framework]] (Neurodiversity/ADHD support).
* From *Working Memory Constraints* $\rightarrow$ Engineer [[Formative Hinge Questions]].
