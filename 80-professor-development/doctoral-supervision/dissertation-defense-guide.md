# Doctoral Viva Voce Preparation: The Master Professor’s Playbook

![Doctoral Viva Colloquium](../../assets/doctoral_viva_colloquium_1789440008026.jpg)

## 1. The Nature of the Doctoral Defense (Viva Voce)
The viva voce is not an interrogation of factual memory; it is a **scholarly peer examination** verifying the candidate’s readiness to enter the international academic community as an independent scholar.

## 2. The 5 Fatal Defense Questions
A distinguished doctoral advisor conducts rigorous mock defenses training candidates to address 5 critical questions:
1. **The "So What?" Question (Significance & Novelty)**:
   * *"Why does this empirical finding matter? What would scholarship lose if this dissertation had never been written?"*
2. **The Epistemological Question (Methodological Rationale)**:
   * *"Why did you select a quantitative over a qualitative approach (or vice versa)? What intrinsic epistemological trade-offs does that choice enforce?"*
3. **The Boundary & Limitations Question (Generalizability)**:
   * *"Where DOES your model apply, and under what specific conditions must it NEVER be generalized?"*
4. **The Counterfactual Question (Self-Critique)**:
   * *"If you restarted this dissertation today with the hindsight you now possess, which experimental parameter would you alter on Day 1?"*
5. **The Disciplinary Trajectory Question (Future Research)**:
   * *"Where does your research agenda lead over the next 5–10 years, and how will it reshape this domain?"*
